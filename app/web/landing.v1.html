A few things you might be wondering

Good questions. Straight answers.

What can I promote with Partizan?+

Websites, apps, bots, SaaS products, online stores and other products with a clear action Partizan can measure.

Do I need to know my target audience already?+

No. Product analysis creates the first audience hypotheses; you can correct them if Partizan misunderstood your product.

Which channels can I use?+

Partizan can research across Meta, Telegram, Reddit, Google Ads and TikTok, while execution depends on the access and capabilities available for each channel.

Can I turn a channel off?+

Yes. Your channel choices are boundaries: Partizan adapts future moves without deleting historical learnings.

How does Partizan know what is working?+

It follows the outcome your product measures, then compares real visits, signups, customers, spend and other observed signals.

Do I need a big advertising budget?+

No. You can start from $10, and the best first move may cost $0.

What is live in this product?+

Research and customer-facing controls are live. Execution remains bounded by the actual channel, account, permission and budget available.
