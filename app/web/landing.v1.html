05 · Questions

What founders usually ask.

Is Partizan an analytics dashboard or an AI consultant?+

Neither. The product is built around execution. It uses analysis to decide what to do next, prepares concrete growth actions, measures the result and feeds that evidence into the next decision.

Do I need a large marketing budget?+

No. Partizan is designed to start small. You define the available budget and what one new customer is worth; Partizan prioritizes the smallest tests that can produce useful signal.

Does it spend money without limits?+

No. You set the acquisition budget, customer-cost limit and allowed channels. Connecting an account or adding money never removes those boundaries.

What kind of products can I use it for?+

Partizan is channel-agnostic. It starts from the buyer and the offer, then searches for viable distribution mechanics — paid, organic, community, creator, partnership or direct.
