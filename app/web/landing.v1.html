Questions

What founders usually ask.

Is Partizan an analytics dashboard or an AI consultant?+

Neither. Partizan researches where customers are, prepares the next useful test, measures what happens and uses the result to decide what to try next.

Do I need a large marketing budget?+

No. You can start the product flow with a budget as small as $10. That does not mean Partizan will spend it on ads — research may point to a $0, assisted or manual first move instead.

Does it spend money without limits?+

No. You set the acquisition budget, customer-cost limit and allowed channels. Connecting an account or adding money never removes those boundaries.

Who is Partizan built for?+

Its primary beachhead is people who already built a product but do not know marketing yet — solo founders, technical founders and small teams without a marketing department.
